Frequently asked questions

How is usage billed?

Per second of container runtime, rounded up to the nearest second. You are not billed while a function is scaled to zero.

Can I set a spending limit?

Yes. Every workspace has a monthly budget you can set in the dashboard; deployments pause when it is reached.

Do you offer reserved capacity?

Enterprise customers can reserve dedicated GPUs for one or three year terms at a discount to on-demand rates.

What payment methods do you accept?

Credit card for Starter and Team; invoicing with net-30 terms for Enterprise.
