Zephyr Compute

We make GPUs boring

Zephyr Compute exists so that machine learning engineers never have to think about a cluster again.

Zephyr Compute was founded in 2023 in San Francisco by a group of engineers who had spent the previous decade building internal ML platforms at large companies. Every one of those platforms solved the same problems: packaging code into containers, finding a GPU, keeping it busy, and tearing it down before finance noticed.

We decided to build that platform once, properly, and offer it to everyone. Today Zephyr runs inference and training workloads for hundreds of teams, from two-person startups to public companies, on GPU capacity across North America, Europe and Asia-Pacific.

We are a team of 42 people, mostly engineers, working from San Francisco, Berlin and remotely. We are backed by Crestline Ventures and Aperture Capital.
